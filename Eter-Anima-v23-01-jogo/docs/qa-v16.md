# v1.6 — Mobile landscape and fullscreen

The game now uses the available dynamic viewport height with no minimum-height clipping. Landscape mode has a 44px header, compact 54px party strip, separate corner HUD, small directional controls, and a map camera scaled for short viewports. Fullscreen is requested from a visible icon with native exit support, WebKit fallback, and best-effort landscape orientation on touch devices. Browser refusal gives a visible instruction; the game remains usable horizontally without fullscreen.

Menus now portal inside the whole game shell, covering header and party strip while open. Landscape navigation occupies a scrollable sidebar; content scrolls independently. Header shortcuts open quests, skills and bag directly. Battle commands occupy the right side, with vertical scrolling at very small heights and no horizontal overflow. Dialogue portraits remain visible; the party strip hides while reading dialogue or cinematics. Landscape title art always uses the horizontal castle asset. Device safe-area insets are respected.

Validation: TypeScript, 556 baseline and 70 origin checks passed. Browser QA at desktop 1363x936, landscape 844x390 and compressed browser viewport 740x260. Native fullscreen entry/exit passed in the top-level game. The test iframe rejected fullscreen; its refusal message was verified. Checked title, four-hero selection, reset confirmation, new Marin solo route, world HUD, direct skills/bag access, sidebar navigation, shop purchase (80 -> 55 credits, 3 -> 4 potions), reading dialogue with both portraits, and battle attack. At 740x260, battle console clientWidth and scrollWidth both equaled 254px: no horizontal overflow. Temporary fixtures removed before the final build. No physical Android/iOS device was available; user testing will verify browser chrome, rotation, and native fullscreen on their phone. Public-by-link audience remains unchanged.
